Scope

The schedule covers the agency's own operating cost and nothing held in trust. Two ledgers report on the same fund number, and only one of them is appropriated, so a figure carried across without its lane is wrong before it is added up.

Everything below is measured against the enacted budget rather than the request, which is why a request-side item can appear here with no dollars beside it.

Method

Every line is traced from the allotment packet to the general ledger and back, so a number that cannot be traced in both directions is dropped rather than estimated. The trace is mechanical: a query, a join, and a reconciliation that either balances to the dollar or fails loudly.

Where the two sides disagree, the disagreement is reported as its own row. Reconciling silently would leave the reader with a clean total and no way to see what was spent to make it clean.

Fund 600

Fund 600 carries two lanes. The appropriated lane is the one the schedule reconciles to; the non-appropriated lane reports on the same number and never nets in. A reader who takes the fund total off a statewide report and drops it here has taken both.

The split is not visible in the fund code, which is the whole difficulty. It lives in the appropriation index beside it.

Allotments

An allotment packet is a plan, not a spend, and it is revised through the biennium. Reading a mid-biennium packet as though it were final is the most common way an out-year figure arrives here overstated.

The revision history is kept, so a figure can be shown as of any date rather than only as of now.

Objects

Object and subobject are where a salary line becomes a benefit line and a contract becomes a service. The mix moves without the total moving, which is why a flat program can still be a program in the middle of a change.

Object A carries staff, object E carries goods and services, and the boundary between them is decided by the coding manual rather than by the transaction.

Reversions

What is not spent by the end of the biennium reverts, and a reversion is a fact about the appropriation rather than about the program. A large one is worth reading twice: it can mean the work did not happen, or it can mean the work was cheaper than the packet assumed.

Both readings are supportable from the ledger alone, so neither is asserted here.

Schedule 9, agency operating detail

Amounts shown are enacted, net of reversions. The appropriated lane of fund 600 reconciles to the dollar, and the schedule beside it does not, by construction rather than by error.

Object detail follows the coding manual in force at the time of the transaction. Subobject rollups are recomputed rather than carried, so a restated year moves here before it moves anywhere else.

From a script

const el = document.querySelector('#sec-4');
Land.scrollerOf(el)                              // #doc, the only thing that moves
Land.mark(el)                                    // 0.28 down, tinted, fading at 4000ms
Land.mark(other, { scroll: false, current: false }) // another member of the set, weaker
Land.mark(row, { ifNeeded: true, tint: false })   // keep it in view, say nothing
Land.clear(document.querySelector('#doc'))       // every mark under here, off
