Where the line is

note Meant to be one line: what the page already implies and cannot fit, so nobody asks where it came from. Nothing tappable, no scrollbar; on touch, tapping the note closes it. Six lines is the ceiling; past it the console says so.
card Anything the reader taps inside, or scrolls: a link, a copy button, a table; and anything a reader might ask the source of, which the card names with a ↗. Carries its ✕ on touch. The rule, once: daisy-alpine/references/mechanics.md, "Notes and cards".
