Why today's row fails

Turn on Show tap targets and switch to Today. The three trailing icons measure about 16 px square. Apple's minimum is 44 px, Google's is 48 dp. They are not small-ish; they are a third of the floor, and the gap-0.5 between them is 2 px, well under the 8 px usually asked for between adjacent targets.

The deeper problem is that they buy one tap. Open the repo and its GitHub link is the sidebar's own name and Config is a row in the list. So the cluster duplicates, on every row, forever, what is one tap away.

What the menu is worth

A menu that only repeats Config and GitHub is not worth a gesture. It earns one by carrying what the row cannot: jumping straight into that repo's Files or Branches, switching to its public/private companion, and copying a browse link. Those are the things you currently cannot do without opening the repo first.

The breadcrumb

Tap a row, and the top bar becomes ⌂ › name ⌄. The house is the route home that the open drawer otherwise hides, at a full 44 px. The name opens the same menu the hold opens, with a visible caret, because here it labels where you are rather than competing with a navigation tap. Dropping mehrlander/ is what pays for it.
