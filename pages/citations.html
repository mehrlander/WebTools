Method

Two halves, neither useful alone

wa-bills holds which bills cite each RCW chapter: 63,880 bills, 1,663,223 citations, 2,822 chapters, extracted in six minutes by regex over the bill XML. The estate index holds which repos discuss each chapter, harvested the same way from their prose. A chapter in both is a thread you can pull.

Why it is sorted this way

By how much the reading repos discuss a chapter, not by how much the corpus cites it. Corpus volume answers a different question and is dominated by procedural furniture: the heaviest-cited chapter is RCW 34.05, the Administrative Procedure Act, at 7,403 bills.

No model was involved

A 150-bill probe measured the alternative: an entity recognizer over bill bodies returns 2% confirmable organization names, headed by RCW and Sec, roughly fourteen hours of model time for a worse answer. The bill XML already tags its sponsors and numbers, and regex reads its citations exactly.

Limits

  • Sections roll up to their chapter, since the chapter is the unit the reading repos name. Section-level detail is in the gitignored shards.
  • Ten heaviest citers per chapter are committed; the full chapter-to-every-bill mapping is 15.8 MB and stays out.
  • Only RCW is aggregated. Session-law and bill-reference citations are extracted but not indexed.
  • The reading-side counts come from markdown prose only, so a repo whose material is PDF or XML is under-represented here.
